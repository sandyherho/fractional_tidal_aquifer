Symbols, units, and reference values
====================================

reference aquifer (illustrative, not calibrated)
------------------------------------------------
  T = 0.05 m^2 s^-1, S_m = 5e-05, S_im = 0.001, beta = 20
  ell = 0.5 m, D_im = 1.93e-06 m^2 s^-1, tau = 129533.7 s = 1.4992 d
  x_* = sqrt(T tau / S_m) = 11381.3 m
  head noise 10 mm in the tidal bands, 50 mm above two days; logging every 600 s

provenance
----------
  The parameter values are round numbers chosen to sit in a plausible
  range for a karstic limestone coastal aquifer with
  decimetre-to-metre matrix blocks. They are not measurements and no
  site is represented. Constituent periods are the standard
  astronomical values; amplitudes are illustrative.

what the results do not depend on
---------------------------------
  The identities for a^2 - b^2 and 2ab, the bound r <= 1, the slab
  floor, the power-law plateau orders, the H^2 invariant and the
  attenuation-only overheight hold for any parameter values.
